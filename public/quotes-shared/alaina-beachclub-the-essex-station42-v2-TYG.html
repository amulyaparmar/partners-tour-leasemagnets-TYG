Timeline & Terms
Two coordinated workstreams
Beach Club + The Essex
  • New-property production. Both communities move through one coordinated planning and scheduling process.
  • Confirm the accompanying service agreement and primary contact for each community.
  • Collect access notes, model availability, renovation priorities, brand assets, and preferred shoot windows.
  • Build property-specific shot lists while keeping the output system consistent.
  • Publish one branded, shareable Tour experience per property.
Station 42
  • Legacy reshoot. Audit the current Tour and identify what should remain, change, or be replaced.
  • Prioritize renovated spaces and the strongest current leasing messages.
  • Capture replacement footage and updated property moments.
  • Rebuild the story around today’s Station 42 experience.
  • Publish the refreshed Tour and replacement launch assets.
Production sequence
01
Agreement. Confirm scope, contacts, and the two-property service agreement.
02
Planning. Gather access notes, brand assets, availability, and priorities.
03
Scheduling. Lock a coordinated production calendar and shot plans.
04
Capture. Film each community against its approved shot list.
05
Review. Share first cuts and one coordinated feedback round.
06
Launch. Publish Tours, branded links, and platform-ready assets.
Property teams provide
  • Shoot-ready model units, amenities, and common areas
  • An on-site contact and access for each production day
  • Advance notice of renovation, construction, or restricted spaces
  • Current logos, leasing URLs, phone numbers, and campaign priorities
  • Shot-list sign-off before production
Working assumptions
  • Beach Club and The Essex are priced at $2,000 each
  • Station 42 is priced at $1,800 as a legacy reshoot
  • Final capture priorities are confirmed during pre-production
  • Material scope changes are reviewed and approved before billing
  • Property teams receive commercial use of final approved assets
