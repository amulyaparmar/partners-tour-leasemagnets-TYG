1. Scope of Work

LeaseMagnets will provide digital marketing technology and production solutions for Revery (the “Property” and the “Services”), including:

  • One professionally produced, guided video tour per scheduled production package;
  • An interactive Video Leasing Agent experience built from the approved tour, property information, FAQs, and calls to action;
  • Three short-form paid and social ad concepts per tour, delivered in platform-ready formats; and
  • LeaseMagnets hosting, lead-capture, delivery, updates, and supporting infrastructure.

Additional services may be provided as mutually agreed upon in writing. Paid media spend, campaign placement, on-camera talent, additional revisions, additional floor plans, photography, Matterport scans, and other services not expressly listed above are excluded unless separately approved in writing.

The license is non-exclusive. LeaseMagnets may grant to others, or reserve for its own use, rights that are the same as or similar to those granted to Customer. This Agreement is personal to Customer, may be used only for Customer’s internal operations, and is non-transferable, non-assignable, and non-sublicensable. With LeaseMagnets’ prior written approval, Customer may permit affiliated entities and authorized service providers to use the Service consistent with this Agreement. Customer remains responsible for the acts and omissions of each party it allows to access or use the Service. Unless otherwise expressly agreed, Customer may use the Service within the United States only.

2. Accessibility and Privacy

At its discretion, LeaseMagnets may use technological and operational methods to understand and support Customer’s use of the Service and to prevent unauthorized use. Customer agrees not to decompile or reverse engineer the Service.

Unless it receives Customer’s prior written consent, LeaseMagnets will not access or use Customer data other than as necessary to facilitate the Service and will not give a third party access to Customer data except to service providers assisting LeaseMagnets in delivering the Service. 3. Term of Agreement

This Agreement applies individually to Revery and to any additional properties confirmed by email for onboarding. It remains in full force and effect on a yearly basis per property. The date the first monthly infrastructure invoice is paid for a property marks the start of its annual renewal cycle. The Agreement automatically renews each year unless written notice of cancellation is provided in accordance with Section 4.

4. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement for an individual property before the start of its next annual cycle with at least 30 days’ advance written notice. If notice is not received before the renewal date, the Agreement renews for another year for that property.

Customer agrees to pay LeaseMagnets a production package fee of $2,500 per tour. The production package includes the guided video tour, the initial Video Leasing Agent build for the approved tour, and three short-form ad concepts.

Customer also agrees to a separate $1,000 travel budget per tour for approved flights, lodging, ground transportation, and related on-site production logistics. Travel is planned in advance and accounted for separately from the creative production fee. Costs beyond this budget require Customer’s written approval.

A deposit of $1,750, equal to 50% of the first-tour production and travel investment, is due upon booking and locks the production date. The remaining first-tour balance is due upon final delivery unless otherwise agreed in writing.

The recurring LeaseMagnets infrastructure fee is $250 per property per month, billed monthly. The first payment is due when the Video Leasing Agent goes live on the Property’s website, with subsequent payments due on the same calendar day each month.

New properties may be added by written confirmation. Each additional property is subject to the same monthly infrastructure fee. Production and travel fees for each additional property or tour will be confirmed before scheduling.

Customer is responsible for providing LeaseMagnets with Google Tag Manager access or direct embed access to the Property website within 14 days after this Agreement is signed. Customer will also provide accurate, approved property information—including pricing, availability, FAQs, policies, and calls to action—for use by the Video Leasing Agent.

Media spend and placement are not included. Additional production costs, including on-camera actors, location fees, permits, extensive travel beyond the approved budget, extra revision rounds, professional photography, floor-plan capture, or Matterport services, require separate written approval.
