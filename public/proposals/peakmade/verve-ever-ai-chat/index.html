LM-AICHAT-VERVE · 05 SEP 2026Draft · commercial terms pending

01Property + Service Overview

LeaseMagnets proposes a 24/7 website leasing assistant for VERVE Charlottesville. AI Chat helps prospects get immediate answers from approved property information and find a clear next step with the leasing team.

Property

Student living near UVA grounds and The Corner, with studios through four-bedroom apartments.

Leasing context

Advertised as coming Fall 2027.
Leasing office: 919 W. Main Street.

Property information: official property website ↗ · reviewed September 5, 2026.

02Included Capabilities

Website chat
A property-branded chat launcher and conversation window with a question input for prospect inquiries.
Property Q&A
Answers about layouts, amenities, location and leasing steps using the property’s approved knowledge base.
Suggested questions
Prompts help prospects begin a conversation about topics such as amenities, the neighborhood or a visit.
Leasing links
Relevant links direct prospects to the property’s existing floor-plan, availability and contact pages.
Leasing-team direction
Questions requiring current information or a person are directed to the property’s published leasing contact.

03Service Scope

For this property and its website, LeaseMagnets will provide:

  1. Configuration. Set up the assistant’s property identity, greeting, prompts and approved leasing links.
  2. Knowledge setup. Organize approved information covering furnished layouts, planned amenities, UVA and Charlottesville context, parking and EV-charging questions, and preleasing next steps. Unconfirmed details require leasing-team confirmation.
  3. Website setup & launch review. Coordinate placement with the website administrator and review sample answers, links and the desktop/mobile experience with the property before launch.

Scope is limited to website AI Chat. Live inventory feeds, confirmed calendar bookings, CRM sync, automated follow-up, video production and advertising require separate written scope.

05Terms & Responsibilities

  1. Property responsibilities. The client provides website access, approved brand assets and accurate property content, designates a content owner, and communicates changes to policies, rates, availability and leasing links.
  2. Service delivery. Launch follows content and placement approval. “24/7” describes the intended availability of the website assistant; it does not establish a human-support schedule, uptime guarantee or leasing-results guarantee.
  3. Answer boundaries. The assistant supplies information and leasing directions. The leasing team confirms changing details, tour arrangements, applications and lease terms.
  4. Changes. Changes to capabilities, integrations, fees, service term or responsibilities require written agreement by both parties. Renewal and cancellation follow the completed terms in Section 04.

06Agreement & Acceptance
