The Package  ·  Indicative pricing per property
01
Lifestyle Launch Film
$2,000

The anchor piece. The property, campus surroundings, restaurants, the life a resident actually gets, ending on a leasing call to action. One anchor film, 60 to 90 seconds, 4K horizontal, plus one vertical cutdown of 15 to 30 seconds for Instagram, TikTok, or paid social. Additional cutdowns at $150 each.

Additional details
Aerials of the property and surrounding area; ground b-roll of dining, campus surroundings, walkability; day and twilight. Creative direction, shot plan, licensed music, sound design, color grade, and titles all included.
02
Property Photography
$1,500

The workhorse. It fills every listing you have. 45 to 60 fully edited images at full resolution and web size, ILS and MLS ready.

Additional details
Model units at 12 to 15 images each, up to two units; amenities 15 to 20; exteriors day and twilight; 5 to 8 aerials. On-site staging direction, full edit and color on every image.
03
FPV Fly-Through
$1,500

The piece people share. One continuous drone run from the street, through the lobby and common areas, into a model apartment: the closest thing to a tour from the couch. 60 to 90 seconds, 4K horizontal plus 9:16 vertical.

Additional details
Flown in house by Meliza, with no subcontracted pilot. Sound design included.
04
Travel & Production & Actors
$2,500

Flights, lodging, and ground transport for a two-day trip per property: one shoot day plus one weather buffer day. All drone work is FAA-licensed, subject to local flight clearance. Indicative flat, all-in pricing per property.
